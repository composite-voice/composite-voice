# Code of Conduct

CompositeVoice is an open source project and we want it to stay that way — welcoming, inclusive, and genuinely useful to everyone who shows up. This document describes the standards we hold ourselves and each other to.

---

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## What we expect

**Behaviour that makes this a good place:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being patient with people who are learning — everyone starts somewhere
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully and offering it kindly
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just yourself
- Showing empathy and assuming good intent

**Behaviour that is not acceptable:**

- Harassment of any kind — public or private
- Deliberately offensive, derogatory, or dismissive comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing private information about another person without their explicit permission (doxxing)
- Trolling or sustained disruption of discussions
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or unwelcome in a collaborative work setting

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review comments, and any other forum where the project community interacts. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behaviour, please report it by:

1. Opening a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/lukeocodes/composite-voice/security/advisories/new) (private, only visible to maintainers)
2. Or contacting the maintainer directly through the contact information in their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lukeocodes)

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality. We will not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

---

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. In response to behaviour that violates this code of conduct, they may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- A private warning with an explanation of why the behaviour was unacceptable
- A request for a public apology
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from the project

The severity of the response will be proportional to the impact of the behaviour.
