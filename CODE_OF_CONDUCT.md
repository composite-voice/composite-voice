# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — as contributors, maintainers, and community members — pledge to make participation in CompositeVoice a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to an open community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our standards

### Behaviour that makes this community thrive

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being patient and encouraging with people who are learning — everyone starts somewhere
- Respecting different viewpoints, experience levels, and approaches to problems
- Offering and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility when you make mistakes, apologising when appropriate, and learning from it
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just yourself
- Showing empathy and assuming good intent

### Behaviour that is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind — public or private
- Offensive, derogatory, or dismissive comments about a person's identity or characteristics
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission (doxxing)
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or sustained disruption of discussions
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or unwelcome in a collaborative work setting

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review comments, the repository wiki, and any other forum where the community interacts. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (for example, at a conference or on social media).

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this code of conduct, please report it. Reports are taken seriously, reviewed promptly, and handled with confidentiality.

**How to report:**

1. Open a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/lukeocodes/composite-voice/security/advisories/new) — this is private and only visible to maintainers.
2. Or contact the maintainer directly through the contact information in their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lukeocodes).

Please do not report code of conduct violations through public issues or pull requests. Public reports can escalate situations and discourage future reporting.

We will not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, and we will not reveal the identity of reporters without consent.

---

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. When a violation is reported or observed, the maintainer will:

1. **Review the report** as quickly as possible — typically within 48 hours.
2. **Reach out privately** to all parties involved to understand what happened.
3. **Take action** proportional to the severity of the behaviour:
   - A private warning with a clear explanation of why the behaviour was unacceptable
   - A request for a public apology
   - Temporary suspension from the project and its spaces
   - Permanent exclusion from the project and its spaces

Decisions will be communicated clearly and, where appropriate, documented.
